## Results
Average FPS: **15.016779** (4475 frames / 298s, `Game time = 4:58`,
  captured verbatim from `FPS.TXT`). **PASS.** Frame-time samples:
  "4474 used, 0 rejected any category" -- clean.

  **Back to the campaign's usual 298s/15.016779fps ceiling-hit
  signature -- NOT the "one second over" pattern the two 486DX2 tiers
  showed on this same card.** This is the confirming data point for the
  tier-boundary theory raised in
  `docs/benchmarks/cirrus-cl-gd5434-486dx2-50-2026-09-05.md`: within the
  Cirrus sweep itself, both 486DX2 tiers (50 and 66) show the
  299s/14.966555fps effect, while both faster tiers (Am5x86-133, this
  leg; POD83, the sweep's first cell) land on the clean 298s/15.016779fps
  figure. A clean boundary between the two 486DX2 tiers and the two
  faster tiers, not scatter -- consistent with a small, fixed
  banked-path cost that only the tightest-margin CPUs fail to fully
  absorb before the integer-second rounding boundary.
1% low: RUNMANIFEST `fps_p50=15.00`, `fps_p95=14.96` -- matches the
  ViRGE+Am5x86-133 leg's `fps_p95` (`14.96`/`14.97`) almost exactly, and
  the frame count matches too. **No video-path effect at all at this
  CPU tier** -- banked vs. LFB makes no measurable difference once the
  CPU has enough headroom, reinforcing that the effect found at the two
  486DX2 tiers is specifically about margin, not something inherent to
  banked rendering that always costs something.
Simulation rate: locked at the 15fps design ceiling, pacer-bound.
Audio underruns: not instrumented. `AUDIO_PRESENT` confirmed multiple
  times mid-run. Tier-matched build, no pitch/tempo mismatch to caveat.
Peak memory: not measured.
