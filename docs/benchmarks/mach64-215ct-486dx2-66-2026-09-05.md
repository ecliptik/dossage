Notes:
- **Closes the campaign's last pre-merge gap.** Every CPU/card pairing
  in the 3-card x 4-CPU matrix now has a datum on `build_sha12=a5e9835f12e7`.
- **Completes the "one second over" effect's full-matrix picture --
  and the pattern across all twelve pairings does not cleanly fit
  either single-factor hypothesis raised so far.** Summary (298s =
  clean signature, 299s = "one second over"; Mach64's 486DX2-50 cell
  has three lives from Round 1-3, the rest have one or two):

  | CPU \\ Card | ViRGE (LFB, 153600 B/frame) | Cirrus (banked, 153600 B/frame) | Mach64 (LFB, 614400 B/frame) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 486DX2-50 | 298s | 299s | mixed: 299s, 298s, 299s (Round 1-3, G1/G2/D3) |
  | 486DX2-66 | 298s | 299s (x2, reproduced) | **298s (this leg)** |
  | Am5x86-133 | 298s | 298s | 299s |
  | POD83 | 298s | 298s | 298s |

  ViRGE never shows the effect, at any CPU tier -- the cleanest part of
  the picture, consistent with it having the most margin (smallest
  framebuffer, LFB). Beyond that, neither "banked path" (Mach64 is LFB
  and still shows it once), "framebuffer size" (Mach64 is largest and
  mostly does NOT show it), nor "CPU tier" (each of the three non-ViRGE
  cards shows a different CPU tier as its exposed one, and 486DX2-50's
  own three Mach64 lives split 2-1) cleanly explains the full table on
  its own. The most defensible reading at this point: overall margin
  (set by some combination of CPU speed and per-card rendering cost)
  determines how close a given pairing sits to the 298/299s rounding
  boundary, and for any pairing close enough to that boundary,
  ordinary per-life timing variance (plausibly tied to the DOS-tick-
  phase-dependent quantization `docs/PACER-TIMING-INVESTIGATION.md`
  already root-caused for the bimodal per-frame clusters) decides which
  side an individual measured life lands on -- which is exactly why
  486DX2-50+Mach64, the pairing with the least margin of the twelve,
  is the only one that has actually flipped between both outcomes on
  repeat lives. Properly separating "which pairings are close to the
  boundary" from "which side chance puts a given life on" would need
  several repeat lives per pairing (most of this table has only one)
  or dedicated per-frame-cost instrumentation -- not done here, since
  nothing in this table threatens the KPI at any cell. Recorded as an
  open characterization question for whoever next has reason to care
  about exact frame-by-frame margins on this port, not an action item.
