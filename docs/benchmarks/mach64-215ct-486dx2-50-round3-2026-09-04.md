## Notes

- **D3 confirms the fix mechanism directly on hardware, not just via
  the fps numbers.** RTC witness: `"RTC elapsed = 299 s (engine
  time(NULL) = 299 s; engine clock gained 0.0%)"` for the real life --
  exact agreement, same as Round 2's D2, a second independent
  confirmation of no clock loss on this rig. Even the trailing 2-Escape
  block agrees exactly (`"RTC elapsed = 9 s (engine time(NULL) = 9 s;
  engine clock gained 0.0%)"` -- unlike Round 2's D2 trailer, which
  showed short-block noise at that duration).
- **Per-stage diagnostic (`STAGEDBG.LOG`) shows the silence-detect fix
  working precisely as designed.** `pump_calls` is not literally zero in
  every window, but is 0-22 calls/window here (`pump_avg` 0.00-0.37ms)
  against Round 2's D2, which spiked to ~110+ calls (~9ms `pump_avg`) in
  exactly 3 of 15 windows -- the loop-point-aligned silence stretches
  that `patches/passage/0041`'s `SDL_HINT_DOS_SILENCE_DETECT=0` hint
  targets. `blowup_avg` holds at ~3.64ms, the same improvement Round 2's
  G/E1 already showed (0040 unaffected by this fix, as expected -- they
  touch different subsystems). 15fps's reduction: over-budget frames 85
  of 3900 total (vs. Round 2 D2's 210), steady-state per-frame work
  ~39.5ms, sleep ~20.0ms, slack ~18.5ms -- **the same regime the
  486DX2-66 showed throughout the original campaign, now reproduced on
  the DX2-50, the port's minimum target hardware.**
- Side note, resolved, no impact on results: during G2's setup,
  `vcctrl-c3` hit a stale/stuck primary-capture ring (a separate KVM
  viewer session had it pinned) -- resolved itself once that viewer's
  pin expired; verified via the second camera in the meantime that
  nothing on the DOS side was affected.
- Same sampler split (2256/85/59, per `15fps`'s cross-cell check) and
  4475-frame lives on every cell this round and Round 2 -- confirms the
  runs are genuinely comparable to each other, not an artifact of
  different play lengths.

## Close-out

- **Patches, permanent on branch `dx2-50-15fps` (`30d3935`)**: `0036`
  (RUNMANIFEST capture re-based on `SDL_GetTicksNS()`), `0038` (removes
  the temporary Round 1 `0037` diagnostic), `0039` (device-native audio
  format, L1c), `0040` (16bpp blowup, L2), `0041` (silence-detect hint,
  the fix this round validates), plus a `Makefile` audio-tier guard.
  `0037` itself does not survive in the series (removed by `0038`); the
  diagnostic/RTC-witness builds (D2, D3) came from a separate scratch
  branch of the vendor tree that never touches `patches/` at all -- the
  shipping configuration's patch series is clean of diagnostic-only
  code.
- **Shipping build fingerprint**: `build_sha12=1d969d876e88` (recomputable
  only from the `dx2-50-15fps` worktree path, per that worktree's own
  `make build-sha12`).
- **Merge decision made, 2026-09-04: MERGED.** `dx2-50-15fps` merged to
  `main` as `2881cfc` (no-ff; the user's explicit "Yes merge"), not
  pushed to the remote. Main's `vendor/passage` was behind the series
  right after the merge (35 commits vs. 41 patches,
  `verify-patches-applied.sh` failed as designed) until the `15fps`
  session ran `make patches`/`make game` there; main now builds clean
  and verified. `main`'s own `build_sha12` differs from this worktree's
  `1d969d876e88` for identical inputs -- the fingerprint embeds the
  checkout path (see "Shipping build fingerprint" above), not a real
  divergence.
- **Still open: whether the shipping `AUDIO_TIER` default stays `high`
  or moves to `low`.** `15fps`'s recommendation: keep `high` -- with
  patch `0039`, both tiers cost the same at runtime on this 8-bit-DAC
  hardware (both collapse to a `memcpy` of U8-mono samples at the file's
  own rate), so the only remaining difference is 22050Hz vs. 11025Hz
  source quality and WAV file size, and the `make stage` guard (from the
  audio-tier-mismatch fix) now makes a tier/file mismatch structurally
  impossible. **Workflow gotcha with that guard, noted 2026-09-04**:
  `make stage` at the default `AUDIO_TIER=high` now refuses with the
  committed low-tier `SONG.WAV` until `make render-music
  AUDIO_TIER=high` has been run, which rewrites
  `vendor/passage/gameSource/music/SONG.WAV` and leaves the vendor tree
  dirty -- `apply-patches.sh` then refuses to reset until it's restored
  with `git checkout`. The guard is doing its job, but a follow-up that
  has `render-music` write to `build/` and `stage` copy from there would
  make the workflow clean.
- **Two items recorded as open, not resolved, so they aren't lost**:
  (1) the ~90s text-mode-to-gameplay startup seen on every Round 2/3
  launch (Round 1 saw ~20-40s) -- `SDL_Init`'s audio or joystick probing
  after a cold boot is suspected, not confirmed. (2) The 512x384-mode
  banding seen on Mach64 when `SDL_HINT_DOS_FORCE_MODE_ID` is omitted
  (Round 2's E0) -- confirmed not a pitch/padding mismatch, true
  mechanism (most likely leftover VRAM/text-mode content) not pinned
  down.
